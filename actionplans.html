Action Plans and It's Impact on People

Economic Issues

Impact on people: Economic issues such as inflation and tariffs cause everything to be more expensive. Inflation quickly increases costs of everyday items daily and tariffs discourage other countries from selling their goods to the U.S.

Action plan: Our attempts to reduce inflation would be to stop/slow the production of new money into the economy in order to slow down inflation. Now this doesn’t fix all the issues of inflation but it’s still a hard hitter. Now of course, tariffs are arguably good for American Businesses, they cause the costs of imported goods/items to increase. Our plan is to not remove tariffs totally but just to reduce them by writing a bill.

Social Issues

Impact on people: Social issues such as poverty and crime greatly impact our society. Especially poverty as the unemployment rate and crime rate had been increasing every year over the past decades.

Action Plan: Addressing these issues by promoting economic growth by increasing people’s wages, provide more jobs to people who don’t have them, and promote programs that will help improve the financial lives of those who are at a disadvantage. Misdirection of Technology

Impact on people: Technology has both positive and negative effects on the country. For starters, technology has a negative effect on certain aspects of education, it is currently harming the environment and the job industry, and generative AI can be responsible for creating propaganda. This all leads to a dystopian society which affects all voters. Additionally, we are in a race with other countries when developing technology which can potentially cause disputes.

Action plan: In some cases, technology has improved our societies and some businesses so we are not looking to remove AI completely. Our plan is to create local tech training programs to open offices in my area to create high paying jobs for residents to make up for the loss of jobs during the automation revolution in the industries. We will also keep up with the races against other countries by upgrading local public services such as installing high speed internet, and using smart systems to improve public safety and transit.
